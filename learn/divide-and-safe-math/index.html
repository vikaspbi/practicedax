DIVIDE and Safe Math

Avoid divide-by-zero surprises in ratios and margins.

DIVIDE

DIVIDE(numerator, denominator[, alternate]) returns BLANK (or alternate) when the denominator is zero — cleaner than raw / for KPIs.

Typical ratios

Margin % = DIVIDE([Profit], [Revenue]). Always decide what "no revenue" should show: blank, 0, or a placeholder.

Try it in the lab

In free practice, try DIVIDE(SUM(Sales[Quantity]), DISTINCTCOUNT(Sales[CustomerID])).
